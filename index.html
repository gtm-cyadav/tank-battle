Full screen on iPhone

iPhone Safari can't go full screen on its own. Add the game to your Home Screen once:

  1. Tap Share (on newer iPhones it sits inside the ··· menu)
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen
  3. Open Tank Battle from your Home Screen

Tip: add it to your Home Screen

Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, and open Tank Battle from there. It then always opens full screen.

Sound

Starts after your first tap. Silent while the game is in the background. On iPhone, the ring/silent switch also mutes it.

Graphics

Low: no shadows, a softer picture and fewer effects, for phones that stutter. Wall and floor detail changes the next time the game opens.

Corner map

Shows the walls and your own tank. Never the other player. While a ping is on, it shows even when switched off.
