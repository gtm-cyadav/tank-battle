Sound

Starts after your first tap. Silent while the game is in the background. On iPhone, the ring/silent switch also mutes it.

Graphics

Low: no shadows, a softer picture and fewer effects, for phones that stutter. Wall and floor detail changes the next time the game opens. If a round stutters for 5 s, the game switches to Low once by itself (Undo on the notice).

Map

Shows the walls and your own tank. Never the other player. While a ping is on, it shows even when switched off.

Aim assist

Hunter only. If you fire while the hider is on your screen and your aim is close, the shot bends a few degrees towards it, with a small lead if it is moving. It never works on a hider you cannot see. Light is about 3 degrees, Medium 6, Strong 10. Hits are still judged on the hider's phone.

Chatter

Speech bubbles from the leaders: watching, bump and poke. Short: the watching line in round 1 only, and one line per bump. Off: none. Taunts and headlines always show.
