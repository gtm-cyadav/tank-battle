Tank Battle

Full screen on iPhone

iPhone Safari can't go full screen on its own. Add the game to your Home Screen once:

  1. Tap Share (on newer iPhones it sits inside the ··· menu)
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen
  3. Open Tank Battle from your Home Screen

Or add it to your Home Screen

Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, and open Tank Battle from there. It then always opens full screen.

Keyboard

Arrows or WASD to drive, Space for the action button, Enter to start.

Leave the game?

You'll go back to the start screen.

Turn your phone sideways.

Tanks do not fit in portrait.
